Signing In

▶
Choose Your RoleSelect the "Customer" or "Shop Owner" tab at the top of the login modal before entering credentials.
▶
Enter CredentialsType your registered email and password, then click "Sign In."

New Users

Creating an Account

+
Switch to Sign UpClick "Sign Up" at the bottom of the modal to reveal the registration form.
+
Fill Your DetailsEnter your Full Name, Contact Number, Email, and a secure Password. Shop Owners also provide Boutique Name and City.

02. The AI Stylist

Prime Selections  ·  Live Top 3 Matches

Personalized Style Recommendations

Astrella's AI ranks every gown against your physical profile and displays the top matches with a confidence score, availability badge, price, and full catalog details.

▶
Select AttributesOn the Home page, choose Body Type, Skin Tone, Event, Face Shape, Age Group, and Gender from the attribute selectors.
▶
Find My FitClick "Find My Fit" to trigger the AI engine. Results are ranked and displayed in real-time.
★
BEST MATCH — 90%Highest compatibility. Silhouette, color, and occasion match your profile best.
★
TOP MATCH & GREAT MATCHStrong alternatives at 85% with different price points, styles, and fabrics.

03. Browsing the Collection

Available Apparel

Search, Filter & Pricing

▶
Search BarType any keyword — gown name, color, or fabric — to instantly filter the collection in real-time.
▶
Occasion FilterUse the Filters button to narrow results by event type: Prom, Wedding, Traditional, Formal, or Themed.
▶
View Merchant ProfileClick "View Profile" on any card to see all apparel offered by that specific boutique owner.
₱
Transparent PricingRental price is shown prominently on each card (e.g., ₱1,500). No hidden fees.
●
Status BadgesAVAILABLE (green), RESERVED (orange), IN-LAUNDRY (blue), IN-USE (amber) — all updated in real-time.

04. Gown Details & Booking

Booking Type A

Reservation

⌖
Select DatesPick Pickup and Return dates from the calendar. Reserved dates (in red) and laundry holds (in blue) are blocked.
⏲
Preferred TimeChoose a pickup time slot within the boutique's operating hours.
✎
PaymentChoose In-Store payment or GCash. GCash requests await payment confirmation before approval.

Booking Type B

Visit & Try-On

⌖
Visit DateSelect a short-duration fitting appointment date. These do not reserve the gown long-term.
⏲
Time SlotPick your preferred in-store time. The owner confirms whether the slot is available.
✓
Submit RequestConfirm the request. Trial bookings appear instantly in "My Bookings" with a "Continue to Book" option if you decide to reserve.

05. My Bookings Dashboard

Booking Management Hub

Tracking & Managing Your Reservations

✎
TrialA confirmed try-on appointment. Use "Continue to Book" to upgrade it into a full reservation.
◦
PendingReservation submitted. Awaiting owner approval or GCash payment verification.
✓
ConfirmedOwner approved. Your pickup date and time are locked in — prepare your ID and payment.
↻
RescheduleClick "Reschedule" to modify your pickup or return date. Changes require owner re-confirmation.
✕
CancelCancel any booking before the pickup date. Confirmed bookings may have cancellation terms.
ⓘ
Booking IDEach booking has a unique reference code (e.g., #13D680) for easy communication with the boutique.

08. Managing Apparel Inventory

Inventory  ·  Manage Apparel

Tracking the Lifecycle of Your Collection

↻
Status LifecycleCycle items through Available → Reserved → In-Use → In-Laundry. Set the Laundry Hold count to block dates for cleaning.
₱
Update PricingEdit the rental price at any time directly from the card. Changes reflect immediately in the catalog.
■
Hide / ShowToggle an item's visibility using the "Hidden" badge. Hidden items are removed from the customer view without deleting the record.
▦
Filter by StatusUse the top tab bar (All, Available, Reserved, In-Use, In-Laundry, Sold Out) to quickly audit specific inventory groups.

09. Managing Bookings

Operations  ·  Booking Verification

Processing Client Reservations

▶
Filter by StatusSwitch between All, Trial, Pending, Confirmed, Completed, and Canceled tabs to focus on requests that need action.
▶
Review the RequestCheck client name, contact, gown, pickup date, return date, total value, and payment method before deciding.
✓
Verify / ConfirmClick "VERIFY" to approve the booking. The customer's dashboard updates instantly from Pending to Confirmed.
✕
Cancel RequestUse "Cancel Request" to reject a booking if there are scheduling conflicts. The customer is notified automatically.
